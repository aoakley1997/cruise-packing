📖 How to play

The aim of the game

Earn points by spotting funny cruise behaviour, making predictions and completing side quests.

👥
1. Choose your player

Each device remembers its own player profile.

📅
2. Select the cruise day

Day 1 is boarding in Brisbane on 22 October; Day 9 is your return to Brisbane on 30 October. Sea and shore days can be edited in Settings.

☀️
3. Daily challenges

Spot the behaviour before claiming the daily challenge. Each player can claim once per day.

⚖️
4. Cruise Courtroom — vote!

Alex, Mum and Dad each cast one private vote: Guilty or Not guilty. Votes stay hidden until all three have voted. The majority option wins and every player who voted for that option earns the courtroom points. Each player may vote only once per day.

🎯
5. Passenger bingo

Each player has a different card per day. Tap squares you spot. Complete a row, column or diagonal for a bonus; each line bonus is awarded once per card.

🧭
6. Side quests

Each quest can be completed once per player. Quest 7 is one competition for the whole cruise, with one winner.

🔮
7. Predictions

Each player submits one prediction per day. At dinner, any player can record the family’s agreed correct or incorrect result.

🏆
8. Leaderboard

Scores sync when connected to the internet. Export a backup occasionally.

Be respectful, don't disturb other passengers, and don't photograph or identify strangers for the game. Firebase syncing requires internet access.
